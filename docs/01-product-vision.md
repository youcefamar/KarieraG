# 01 — Product Vision

## Mission
Help Algerian students find the right professional training, compare institutes
with confidence, and build a clear learning/career roadmap — guided by AI.

## Problem
Training offers in Algeria are fragmented across Facebook pages, word of mouth,
and institute websites. Students struggle to compare quality, price, and
relevance, and have no personalized guidance toward a career goal.

## Target users
- **Students** — discover, compare, and enroll in courses; get AI guidance.
- **Trainers** — publish and manage courses, see demand signals.
- **Institute owners** — manage an institute profile and its trainers/courses.
- **Admins** — moderate content, verify institutes, oversee the platform.

## Value proposition
- Personalized **AI course advisor** based on the student's goal and background.
- **Semantic search** that understands intent, not just keywords.
- **Course match score** and **learning roadmap** to plan a career path.
- Trustworthy comparisons via **reviews** and **verified institute badges**.

## Differentiators
- Local-first: Algerian categories, pricing, languages (FR/AR), local institutes.
- AI/RAG grounded in real catalog data (courses, institutes, reviews, FAQs).
- API-first platform reusable by mobile app and a future web dashboard.

## Success metrics (north stars)
- Student activation (completed profile + first enrollment).
- Search → course-detail → enrollment conversion.
- Advisor usefulness (accept rate of recommended courses).
- Institute retention and course publishing rate.

## Non-goals (for now)
- Becoming an LMS / content delivery platform.
- Live video classes.
- General-purpose chatbot unrelated to the catalog.
